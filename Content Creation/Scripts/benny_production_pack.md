# Benny the Bunny: Production Pack v2 (3 Songs, Newton's Laws)

## TECHNICAL SPEC (all videos)
- **Resolution:** 2K = **2560 x 1440** (16:9). Frame rate **30 fps**. Export H.264 MP4, audio AAC 48 kHz stereo.
- **Safe area:** keep key action inside the central 90% of the frame (about 2304 x 1296).
- **Note:** if you need cinema 2K instead, use 2048 x 1080.
- **Render settings:** composition width 2560, height 1440. Render stills at the same size.

## SERIES STYLE SHEET

**Characters**
- **Benny the Bunny:** round, soft white-grey, big shiny eyes, pink inner ears, yellow scarf. Non-verbal: giggles, "hop hop", gasps. Says only short lines in the hook ("Can YOU...?").
- **Baby Bunnies (x3):** tiny versions of Benny in pastel scarves (pink, mint, lilac). They appear in the mid-video surprise of every episode.
- **Tilly the Turtle** (Video 3): green shell, orange helmet.
- **Red Ball:** bright red, one white star. **Big Blue Ball** (Video 2): striped, large.

**Palette:** meadow green #7ED957, sky #8FD3FF, ball red #E63946, scarf yellow #FFD166, cream light #FFF4D6, accents pink #FFB5C8, mint #B8F2E6, lilac #D7C6FF.

**Look:** soft painted 3D meadow, rounded shapes, no sharp edges, no on-screen text.

**Voice:** warm, smiling narrator and a sung chorus. Slow, clear words (~110 wpm). Kid-friendly sing-along tone.

**Music:** ukulele + xylophone + light hand claps, ~95 BPM, same melody motif across all three songs so the series feels connected.

**Series catchphrases**
- Hook line: **"Can YOU...?"** (a question to the child in the first 3 seconds)
- Closing: **"Bye-bye, friends. Hop, hop, hooray!"**

## RETENTION RULES (from the MrBeast-style review, adapted for kids)
1. **Hook in the first 3 seconds:** start mid-action with a question to the child.
2. **New shot every 3-4 seconds:** moderate cuts, not frantic.
3. **Mid-video surprise** at the halfway point (Baby Bunnies arrive).
4. **Repeat one chant** at least twice, with clap/shout moments.
5. **One idea per video**, shown before it is said.
6. **Parent-friendly:** no flashing, no sudden loud sounds, calm final 3 seconds.
7. **Title promise = first scene:** each video's opening shows exactly what the title says.

## VIDEO 1: THE SLEEPY BALL SONG (1st Law) | 40 sec
**Title:** "Benny's Rolling Ball Song"
**Chorus (chant):** *"Still stays still, clap clap! Rolling keeps rolling, whoosh!"*

### Song
> Sleepy ball, sleepy ball, sitting on the grass,
> Not a **move**, not a **move**, till a push comes past!
> Push it, push it, off it goes,
> **Roll, roll, roll**, away it goes!
> Still stays still, **clap clap!**
> Rolling keeps rolling, **whoosh!**

### Screenplay and shots
| Time | Shot | Action | Camera |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:03 | 1 | Ball zooms past camera and freezes. Benny pops in: "Can YOU make the ball move?" | Close-up, quick whip-pan, shallow focus |
| 0:03-0:07 | 2 | Song lines 1-2. Sleepy ball with "zzz"; butterfly lands on it. | Wide, slow push-in |
| 0:07-0:10 | 3 | Benny shrugs, ball does not move. **Pause 2 sec.** | Close-up on ball, locked |
| 0:10-0:14 | 4 | Benny: "Let's push!" Pauses for the child to say "push!" | Medium, eye-level |
| 0:14-0:18 | 5 | Kick! Ball rolls. Song lines 3-4. | Low-angle side tracking |
| 0:18-0:22 | 6 | **Mid-video surprise:** three Baby Bunnies pop out of the grass and cheer. | Medium wide, pop-in bounce |
| 0:22-0:28 | 7 | Ball keeps rolling. Benny and the babies hop alongside, singing. | Tracking, gentle sway |
| 0:28-0:32 | 8 | Ball slows and stops at the fluffy hill. | Side wide, locked |
| 0:32-0:37 | 9 | Chorus with claps and a "whoosh" gesture. | Medium, slight orbit |
| 0:37-0:40 | 10 | Wave: "Bye-bye, friends. Hop, hop, hooray!" Calm music. | Warm close-up, slow pull-out |

### Narration and dialogue
- Benny: "Can YOU make the ball move?"
- Narrator (whisper): "The ball is sleeping." 
- Narrator: "Let's push!" (Benny and child)
- Narrator (excited): "Whoosh! Now it's rolling!"
- Sung chorus, then closing line.

**SFX:** soft "zzz" chime, grass rustle, *thwip* kick, rolling whoosh, soft *pomf*, baby-bunny giggles, claps.

### Direction
- The ball must be perfectly still during the pause. Stillness is the lesson.
- The kick is gentle and cute. Ball slows gradually so "something stops it" is visible.
- Baby Bunnies enter with a bouncy, joyful pop, then follow Benny's actions.

### Cinematography
- 2560 x 1440, soft morning sun, warm rim light on fur, long gentle shadows.
- Eye level for kid-height feel; one low angle for the kick; calm locked shots for pauses.

## VIDEO 2: PUSH HARDER SONG (2nd Law) | 45 sec
**Title:** "Benny's Big Push Song"
**Chorus (chant):** *"Little tap, little roll. Big push, big whoosh!"*

### Song
> Little tap, little roll, **tap, tap, tap**,
> Big push, big whoosh, **clap, clap, clap**!
> Heavy ball, heavy ball, hard to move,
> **Push, push, push**, Benny, YOU!
> Little tap, little roll,
> Big push, big **whoosh**!

### Screenplay and shots
| Time | Shot | Action | Camera |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:03 | 1 | Red ball zooms across the screen. Benny: "Can YOU push it far?" | Close-up whip-pan |
| 0:03-0:07 | 2 | Little tap. Ball rolls a short way and stops. Song line 1. | Close-up on paw and ball |
| 0:07-0:09 | 3 | Benny looks at the ball, looks at us. **Pause 2 sec.** | Medium, locked |
| 0:09-0:14 | 4 | Big push! Wind-up, ears blow back. Ball zooms far away. Song line 2. | Low-angle slow-motion wind-up, whip-pan |
| 0:14-0:18 | 5 | Benny shades his eyes: the ball is tiny in the distance. | Long lens, ball tiny in background |
| 0:18-0:23 | 6 | **Mid-video surprise:** Baby Bunnies each push a mini ball: tiny pushes, tiny rolls. | Wide, playful pop-in |
| 0:23-0:30 | 7 | Big blue ball arrives. Benny pushes, puffing, and it moves only a little. Song lines 3-4. | Wide side shot |
| 0:30-0:35 | 8 | Benny and the babies push together. Ball rolls farther. Cheers. | Medium wide, tracking |
| 0:35-0:41 | 9 | Chorus with claps and "whoosh" arm swing. | Medium, slight orbit |
| 0:41-0:45 | 10 | Benny flops down happily. Wave: "Bye-bye, friends. Hop, hop, hooray!" | Warm close, slow pull-out |

### Narration and dialogue
- Benny: "Can YOU push it far?"
- Narrator: "A tiny tap... a tiny roll."
- Narrator: "Now a BIG push... whoosh!"
- Narrator (funny): "The big ball is heavy. It needs more push!"
- Sung chorus, then closing line.

**SFX:** *tok* tap, short roll, big *whoosh*, wind in ears, low *thunk* of the heavy ball, giggles, claps.

### Direction
- Show contrast clearly: tiny tap = tiny paw motion; big push = full-body effort.
- Heavy ball is visibly bigger and slower; Benny's face scrunched (funny, never frustrated).
- Teamwork beat (shot 8) reinforces "more push, more go."

### Cinematography
- 2560 x 1440, bright midday light.
- Big blue ball contrasts with the red ball and green meadow.
- Shot 4 slow-motion wind-up, then a fast whip-pan on release.

## VIDEO 3: PUSH-BACK FRIENDS SONG (3rd Law) | 50 sec
**Title:** "Benny and Tilly's Push-Back Song"
**Chorus (chant):** *"Push, push back! Boing and whee!"*

### Song
> Push the wall, push the wall, **boing, boing, boing**!
> The wall pushes back, **boing, boing, boing**!
> Push your friend, push your friend, **whee, whee, whee**!
> You go back, they go back, **whee, whee, whee**!
> Push, push back,
> **Boing and whee**!

### Screenplay and shots
| Time | Shot | Action | Camera |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:03 | 1 | Ball bounces off a wall straight to camera. Benny: "Can YOU see it come back?" | Close-up, bounce toward lens |
| 0:03-0:07 | 2 | Benny throws the ball at the wall. Song line 1. | Side wide, path of ball clear |
| 0:07-0:11 | 3 | Boing! Ball bounces back. Benny catches it, amazed. Song line 2. | Side wide, then close on Benny |
| 0:11-0:13 | 4 | Benny scratches his head: "How?" **Pause 2 sec.** | Medium, locked |
| 0:13-0:18 | 5 | Tilly the Turtle arrives on a skateboard. Benny steps on his own. | Wide, gentle tracking |
| 0:18-0:23 | 6 | Gentle push: Tilly rolls one way, Benny the other. Song lines 3-4. | Locked two-shot, both rolling apart |
| 0:23-0:29 | 7 | **Mid-video surprise:** Baby Bunnies line up. The first pushes the second, and they bounce down the line like dominoes. | Wide, playful follow |
| 0:29-0:35 | 8 | Everyone rolls and bounces in different directions, laughing. | Medium wide, slight orbit |
| 0:35-0:44 | 9 | Chorus with claps and "boing / whee" gestures. | Medium, warm light |
| 0:44-0:50 | 10 | All wave. Ball bounces between friends. "Goodnight, friends. Hop, hop, hooray!" Calm music. | Warm medium, slow pull-out |

### Narration and dialogue
- Benny: "Can YOU see it come back?"
- Narrator (amazed): "Boing! The ball pushed the wall, and the wall pushed back!"
- Narrator: "Whee! Tilly goes one way... Benny goes the other!"
- Narrator (warm): "When you push, it pushes back too."
- Sung chorus, then closing line.

**SFX:** *boing*, skateboard roll, giggles, slow "oooh" (Tilly), twinkle at the end.

### Direction
- Show both directions of movement clearly and at the same time.
- Tilly's slow reactions are funny and sweet.
- Pushes are gentle and playful. Keep it safe.

### Cinematography
- 2560 x 1440, late-afternoon golden hour, a bit warmer than Video 1 for a "goodnight" feel.
- Side-on wide shots for the wall bounce so the path in and back is obvious.
